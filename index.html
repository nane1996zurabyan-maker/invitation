How It All Began

It was a quiet autumn afternoon in Paris when our paths first crossed — a shared glance over coffee, a conversation that felt like it had been waiting for us all along. What began as friendship slowly bloomed into something deeper, something timeless.

Through travels across Europe, quiet Sunday mornings, and dreams whispered under starlit skies, we discovered that home is not a place — it is a person. And we found ours in each other.

Now, surrounded by those we love most, we invite you to witness the next chapter of our story.
